FAQs

Frequently asked questions.

Can't see your answer? Call the office — we'd rather talk it through than have you guess.

0161 509 6152

What areas do you serve for delivery services?

We're based in Manchester and cover Salford, Stockport, Trafford, Didsbury and Chorlton as our local heartland. From there we deliver nationwide across England, Scotland and Wales, with international shipping available on request.

How quickly can you deliver my package?

For same day work we're usually at your collection point within 60 minutes of booking in Greater Manchester, then drive direct with no depots or sorting hubs. Overnight and express deliver the next working day; scheduled runs go at a fixed time you choose.

What types of items can you ship?

Parcels, documents, pallets, machine parts, retail stock, e-commerce orders, fragile and high-value goods, and household items for home moves. We can't carry hazardous goods, livestock, illegal items or unpackaged loose liquids. If you're unsure, send a photo and we'll confirm.

Are my goods insured while in transit?

Yes. Every job carries Goods in Transit insurance as standard, alongside full public liability cover. Higher-value consignments can be covered on request — just tell us the declared value when you book.

Do you offer business accounts and credit terms?

We do. Regular clients can open a business account with consolidated monthly invoicing, agreed credit terms, fixed rates on repeat routes and a named account contact who knows your sites.

How do I get a quote, and how is pricing worked out?

Send collection and delivery postcodes, vehicle size and timing, and we'll come back within 15 minutes during working hours. Pricing is based on distance, vehicle size, timing and any loading help required — and the price we quote is the price you pay.

Need it there today?

Tell us the two postcodes. We'll tell you the price and the time — in 15 minutes.
